      Turn guide

      MustOnce a turnAny numberTeam limit

      Your turn

      All players take their turns at the same time, then the boss goes.

      1. Draw a card Must

        If your deck is empty you keep playing and just skip the draw. With Rule help on, the app draws it for you.

      2. Do any of these, in any order
        • Any Put Basic Pokémon on your Bench, up to 5.
        • Any Evolve your Pokémon. Not on your first turn, and not a Pokémon that came into play or already evolved this turn.
        • Once Attach an Energy card from your hand to one of your Pokémon.
        • Any Play Items. Attach Tools, one per Pokémon.
        • Once Play a Stadium. Only one can be in play, and it affects every player and the boss.
        • Team Play Supporters. The whole team can play as many as there are players each turn. One player can use several, and a Supporter can help a teammate, like Professor's Research drawing for someone else.
        • Once Retreat your Active Pokémon by discarding Energy to pay its Retreat Cost.
        • Any Use Abilities, following each card's own limits.
      3. Attack Once

        Optional, and allowed on your first turn. Attacking ends your turn. Click your Active Pokémon and pick an attack: its damage goes into the Raid damage panel, and with Rule help on your turn ends.

      Check with your team first before playing a card that affects "each player", like Iono. It happens to everyone at the same time.

      Damage and the boss

      • Add up every player's damage, plus 10 if the boss is Poisoned or 20 if Burned, minus anything it healed, plus any damage it did to itself.
      • Divide by 10 × number of players and round down. That many boss cards go to the Lost Zone at the end of the boss's next turn.
      • Weakness and Resistance are ignored.

      Boss turn

      • The boss draws one card per player, plus one, and plays each as it's drawn. It never holds a hand.
      • Tools attach (replacing the old one), Stadiums go into play, Energy attaches, Supporters resolve.
      • It attacks every player's Active Pokémon at once. Its attack costs count as any Energy and are paid by discarding.
      • Special Conditions on the boss only last one turn.

      Knock Outs and winning

      • When your Pokémon is Knocked Out, you take Prize cards: 1, or 2 for most ex and V, or 3 for VMAX and Mega ex. Then promote a new Active Pokémon.
      • You're out of the game when you take your last Prize card.
      • Players win when the boss's deck and discard pile are all in the Lost Zone. The boss wins when every player is out, including on the same turn the players would have won.

      Using Raid Table

      • Drag cards between zones. Drop a Pokémon on a Pokémon to evolve, anything else to attach.
      • Click a card for its actions and a large preview. Click the deck to draw, a Prize card to take it.
      • D draws, Ctrl+Z undoes, ? opens this guide.
